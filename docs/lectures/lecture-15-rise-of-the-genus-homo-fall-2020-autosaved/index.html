Slide 3

  • Zinjanthropus boisei
  • Gave confirmation to the dating of fossils in S. Africa
  • Leakeys decided this was the toolmaker, based on association
Notes:

recall that dating of fossils in East Africa is often based on fossil layers interspersed with datable volcanic tuffs (ash) South African dating usually relies on the less accurate method of faunal correlation (biostratigraphy)

Slide 4

  • BUT…
  • Nov 4, 1960
  • Jonathan Leakey
  • “Johnny’s Child”
  • same place as Zinj
  • recovered:
  • mandible, parietals, all lower and some upper teeth, 21 hand and wrist bones
  • smaller cheek teeth
  • relatively larger front teeth
  • larger braincase
  • more gracile in appearance
  • 10-12 years old

Slide 5

  • OH 7
  • Published in 1974
  • estimated a 674 cc brain capacity
  • TWICE the brain size of Australopithecus
  • bigger than Zinj (530cc)
  • smaller teeth
  • hand bones resembled modern humans

Slide 6

  • Who made the tools?
  • BINGO! We Have a Winner!!
  • Obviously tool-maker was the one with the bigger brain
  • Hand bones showed human-like adaptations
  • (no Paranthropus hand bones)

Slide 7

  • BUT…
  • Was the brain big enough to be human?

Slide 8

  • Cerebral Rubicon
  • Early in 1900s, Arthur Keith
  • minimum 750cc brain to be human
  • modern brain = 1200-1600cc
  • ½ between humans and gorillas
  • 1940s, Wilfred Le Gros Clark
  • minimum 700cc brain
  • based on the state of fossils at the time

Slide 9

  • Problem…
  • Johnny’s Child (OH7) = 674cc
  • did not “qualify” by Le Gros Clark standard of 700 cc
  • convinced this tool maker was human
  • What to do?

Slide 10

  • Solution!!
  • LOWER the STANDARD!!
  • evolutionary equivalent of curving grades
  • Leakeys set minimum brain size to 600cc
  • OH7 had a brain size of 657cc
  • Now it qualified!
Notes:

Lower the Standard… essentially the evolutionary equivalent of grading on a curve…

both of these creatures share increased brain size, reduced prognathism, more gracile appearance, size reduction of the post-canine teeth
