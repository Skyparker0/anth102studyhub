  • Hierarchical social system with rigid social classes

Notes:

Greek belief in Demiurg or Prime Mover as organizer of everything. Irreducible complexity Simpler version could not have existed Eyes are too complex – retina, lens, rod, cones, connection to brain Mousetrap Counter – Assumes that all parts must have had their terminal purpose at the beginning Specified Complexity If something is complex and specified, then must have come from an intelligent designer Alphabet letter is specified but not complex. Random letters is complex but not specified. Shakespeare is both Fine-tuned Universe Everything is exactly right for us to exist. If elements were different, we wouldn’t be here.

Slide 19

  • Uniformitarianism
  • Uniformity of Law
  • Natural laws constant in space and time
  • Uniformity of Process
  • Past events should be explained with current processes
  • Uniformity of Rate
  • Change is slow and steady
  • Uniformity of State
  • Change is non-directional and non-progressive
