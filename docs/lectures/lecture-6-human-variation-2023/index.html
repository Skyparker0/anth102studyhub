Slide 27

  • Among mammals of similar size:
  • mammal with a linear shape loses heat more rapidly than the mammal with a nonlinear shape.
  • Bergman’s Rule

Slide 28

  • Allen’s Rule:
  • Mammals in cold climates have short, bulky limbs
  • Minimizes heat loss
  • Mammals in hot climates have long, slender limbs
  • Maximizes heat loss
Notes:

Alphonse Riesnfeld Compared genetically similar rats Raised in cold or warm Colder rats developed shorter limbs Colder had narrower nasal openings, increased facial width
