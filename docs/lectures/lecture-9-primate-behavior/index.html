Slide 4

  • Behavior
Notes:

Behavior is anything an organism does that involves action in response to internal or external stimuli. The response of an individual, group, or species to its environment. These responses may be deliberate, though not necessarily the result of conscious decision-making.

Slide 5

  • Behavioral Ecology
  • Environment
  • Reproduction
  • Survival
Notes:

The study of the evolution of behavior, emphasizing the role of ecological factors as agents in natural selection. Behaviors that have favored the survival or reproduction of individuals in particular environments Understand WHY we see behavior Predict behavior from evolutionary past and/or ecology

  • Sociobiology
